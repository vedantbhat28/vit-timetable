Your roll number is safe

  1. Your roll number is saved only in this browser’s local storage, on your device.
  2. It is never sent to Supabase or to any other server, and no database holds it.
  3. The foreign language check runs entirely on your device.
  4. The list it checks against is built into this page and has only branch, division and roll numbers — no student names.
  5. Whether you were found in that list is worked out on your device. The only thing recorded is an anonymous count of how many visitors have a foreign language course — never your roll number, name or which language.
  6. The usual visit statistics (class, device type) never include your roll number or which language you have.
  7. Nothing in this feature shares it with anyone.
  8. To delete it, clear the box and continue, or clear this site’s data in your browser.
  9. It does not follow you to another phone or browser; you would enter it again there.
  10. It is optional — skip it and the timetable works exactly as before.

Meet Bro connect

Experimental · Beta

Bro connect is built to answer one question: when are you and a friend both free? Add one or more friends (branch, division, lab batch, and whether they take German) and it lines up all the weekly timetables and highlights the best time slots to hang out. You can leave your own timetable out to compare just two friends, and save everything as a PDF with the floppy-disk button.

It’s a new, experimental feature, so results can be wrong or incomplete. It only compares the regular weekly timetable, so holidays, exams and one-off changes are not considered. Please double-check with your friend.

Your friends’ class details, and the PDF you save, stay on this device. Only anonymous usage counts (like how often the feature is opened or used) are recorded, never roll numbers or names.

Find a teacher Experimental

Faculty from the FY timetable are grouped by subject. Tap any name plate to see which room that teacher is in right now and the room and time of their next four periods. The search box on the side still lets you look a teacher up by name.

Campus labels are inferred from the branch: CS, CS-AIML, CS-AI and IT are at Bibwewadi Campus; all other branches are shown as Kondhwa Campus.

This is an experimental timetable helper, not live location tracking. It cannot account for attendance, substitutions, holidays, or last-minute changes—please verify before visiting.
